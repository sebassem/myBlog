In addition to cloud transformation, I help customers architect AI-driven solutions, ensuring they leverage the cloud effectively for innovation, scalability, security and resiliency. My expertise extends beyond technical implementation as I actively lead and contribute to high-impact initiatives like the Cloud adoption and the Well-architected frameworks, Azure Landing Zones, Azure Arc Jumpstart, Azure Verified Modules, and FinOps toolkit. I am also deeply involved in thought leadership, spearheading internal and external initiatives, publishing field content, accelerators, and articles, and sharing insights through webinars, community events, user groups, and large-scale conferences such as Microsoft Ignite.
